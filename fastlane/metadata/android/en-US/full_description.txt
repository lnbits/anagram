Anagram is a private messaging client for the Nostr protocol. Connect directly to Nostr relays and choose which relays the app uses.

Features include:
<ul>
<li>Create a Nostr account or sign in with an existing identity</li>
<li>Exchange encrypted one-to-one messages</li>
<li>Create and join private group conversations</li>
<li>Review new chat requests before accepting, blocking, or deleting them</li>
<li>Search chats and react to, edit, forward, or delete messages</li>
<li>Manage contacts, profile details, relays, themes, and notifications</li>
<li>Upload media through a configurable Blossom server</li>
</ul>

On Android, optional background notifications maintain direct connections to your configured Nostr relays using a foreground service. The service can restart after device boot when enabled. Notifications are delivered directly from Nostr relays without Firebase Cloud Messaging or Google Play Services.

Anagram contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.
