Contact support

For help or privacy requests, email vlad@lnbits.com.

Bug reports and feature requests

You can open an issue on GitHub or check existing reports. Creating an issue requires a GitHub account.

Include your app version, device and operating system, what you expected, what happened, and steps to reproduce the problem. If it concerns a connection, include the affected relay's public URL.

GitHub issues are public. Never post your private key (nsec), recovery words, signer pairing secrets, private messages, or unreviewed diagnostic exports. Redact screenshots before sharing them.

iPhone and iPad calls

  • Keep Anagram open to receive messages and calls. The iOS app does not provide closed-app notifications or lock-screen answering.
  • Audio and video calls require iOS/iPadOS 18.4 or newer and compatible media support. Allow microphone access, and camera access for video.
  • If a call cannot connect, check the selected relays and network connection. Settings → Relays → Iroh lets you review the call relay pool.

Keys and local data

Anagram cannot recover a lost private key. Keep your key backup safe and never send it to support. Settings → Log out clears saved sign-in credentials and local app data; it does not remove copies held by relays, media hosts, or other people.

Read the privacy policy for details about media uploads, service providers, and deletion limits.
