# Asset provenance

This document records the origin and distribution rights for assets shipped with Anagram and used in its F-Droid store listing.

The upstream LNbits project maintainer has confirmed that LNbits owns or has permission to distribute the first-party graphics, screenshots, visible names, profile images, artwork, and conversation content listed below. Unless a separate license is identified, these assets are distributed with Anagram under the repository's [MIT license](../LICENSE).

## Application and brand graphics

| Assets | Source and rights |
| --- | --- |
| `public/nostr_chat.png`, `public/nostr_chat.svg`, `public/favicon.ico`, `public/loading.svg` | First-party Anagram application graphics, distributed under the repository's MIT license. |
| `fastlane/metadata/android/en-US/images/icon.png` | Store copy of `public/nostr_chat.png`; both files are byte-identical and use the same MIT permission. |
| `public/lnbits.svg` | LNbits brand asset included with authorization from LNbits and distributed with this repository. |
